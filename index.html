QLD SEQ Public Transport Interactive Map

Unofficial product. This map is not produced, endorsed or maintained by Translink or the Queensland Government. It is an independent visualisation of Translink open data, provided for information and consultation only. Routes, stops and services change — always check translink.com.au or the MyTranslink app before travelling. No warranty is given as to accuracy or completeness.

Bus route numbering zones show where each three-digit route series predominantly stops (a rule of thumb, not exact boundaries).

How to use: search a route to see its path and stops in order; search a station, stop, street or suburb to jump there. Tap any station or stop (bus stops appear when zoomed in) to see the routes serving it. Show all routes draws every route from that stop; tap any line or route to open it, and use ‹ to return to the stop. The buttons under the search bars turn layers on and off and the half-circle button switches between light and dark maps.

Timetable data: Translink GTFS feed of (route paths simplified). Contains Translink / Queensland Government open data. Base map © OpenStreetMap contributors (Open Database Licence), rendered with Protomaps and MapLibre.
