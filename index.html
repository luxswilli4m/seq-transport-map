QLD SEQ Public Transport Interactive Map

Unofficial product. This map is not produced, endorsed or maintained by Translink or the Queensland Government. It is an independent visualisation of Translink open data, provided for information and consultation only. Routes, stops and services change — always check translink.com.au or the MyTranslink app before travelling. No warranty is given as to accuracy or completeness.

Bus route numbering zones show where each three-digit route series predominantly stops (a rule of thumb, not exact boundaries).

How to use: search a route to see its path and stops in order; search a station, stop, street or suburb to jump there. Tap any station or stop (bus stops appear when zoomed in) to see every route serving it with its next departure and the rest of today's scheduled times (scroll sideways); tap a route badge to open that route. Show all routes draws every route from that stop, with white arrows showing the direction of travel away from it; tap any line or route to open it, and use ‹ to return to the stop. Each button under the search bars cycles through on, off and only this layer (a third click brings the other layers back as they were); the half-circle button switches between light and dark maps. Tap ☆ on a stop or route to save it; ★ Stops and ★ Routes show your saved ones (saved on this device only). Saved routes open a full timetable with every stop, Earlier/Later and a day picker, plus live vehicles and updates. In the Next column, green times are live predictions, amber means running late, and grey is the scheduled time.

What the colours mean: busways are purple (Northern), teal (South East) and orange (Eastern); thinner sections run on ordinary roads. Striped zone areas are where stops from more than one route series overlap. The ⚠ Alerts button turns orange when Translink has posted alerts, tagged Disruption (unplanned, including cancelled trips) or Planned works. Your saved stops and routes, and the ★ buttons, turn orange when an alert affects them, red when a trip has been cancelled and hasn't started yet (red clears once its start time passes), and half red, half orange for both. School routes lists school-only buses, which can share numbers with everyday routes. Journey planner ↗ opens Translink's official journey planner in a new tab; this map does not plan trips itself.

Updates: routes, timetables, bus zones and busways refresh automatically every week from Translink's data, once changes are actually running. How it works and how to update it by hand (for example adding a new busway station) is explained on the project page.

Timetable data (scheduled times, Brisbane time): Translink GTFS feed of (route paths simplified). Live vehicle positions, arrival predictions, cancellations and service alerts: Translink GTFS-Realtime, refreshed every 15–30 seconds.
